Welcome to Kayangan Lake

About Kayangan Lake

Kayangan Lake is renowned for its stunning scenery and crystal-clear waters. It’s a popular spot for both locals and tourists looking to experience the natural beauty of Coron.

Location

Activities

1. Swimming

Enjoy a refreshing swim in the lake's pristine waters after paying the entrance fee.

Fee: Free once you’ve paid the entrance fee

2. Snorkeling

Rent snorkeling gear to explore the underwater beauty of Kayangan Lake.

Fee: Gear rental typically costs PHP 150-300

3. Hiking

Hike to the viewpoints for breathtaking panoramic views of the lake and surrounding landscapes.

Fee: Free after paying the entrance fee

4. Photography

Capture stunning photos of the lake’s views. Some viewpoints may have a small fee.

Fee: Free, though some viewpoints may have a small fee

Welcome to Lake Danao

About Lake Danao

Lake Danao is a stunning natural destination in Leyte, known for its tranquil waters and beautiful scenery. Visitors can enjoy a variety of activities, making it an ideal spot for nature lovers.

Location

Activities

1. Boating

Rent a paddle boat or kayak and explore the serene waters of Lake Danao. It's a perfect way to enjoy the beauty of nature.

Fee: PHP 300-700 for rental

2. Fishing

Try your hand at fishing in the tranquil waters. Equipment rental is available, making it accessible for all skill levels.

Fee: PHP 100-300 for equipment rental

3. Nature Walks

Enjoy the beauty of the surroundings on a leisurely nature walk. It's a great way to relax and appreciate the natural environment.

Fee: Free after paying the entrance fee

4. Picnicking

Bring a picnic and enjoy the day by the lake. There are designated picnic spots, but check for any associated fees.

Fee: Free, but check for designated picnic areas

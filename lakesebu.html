Welcome to Lake Sebu

About Lake Sebu

Lake Sebu is known for its stunning landscapes, vibrant culture, and exciting activities. Here you can enjoy zip-lining, boat tours, fishing, and explore the beautiful waterfalls.

Activities

1. Zip-lining

Experience the thrill of zip-lining over the lush landscapes of Lake Sebu. Soar through the air and enjoy breathtaking views of the surroundings.

Fee: PHP 300-500 per ride

2. Fishing

Try your hand at fishing in the tranquil waters of Lake Sebu. Rent equipment and enjoy a peaceful day on the lake, perfect for both beginners and experienced anglers.

Fee: Equipment rental costs PHP 100-500

3. Waterfalls Tour

Explore the stunning Seven Falls, a breathtaking series of waterfalls located near Lake Sebu. Enjoy a guided tour that includes hiking and zip-lining opportunities.

Entry Fee: PHP 50-100

Zip-line Fee: PHP 300 (additional)

4. Boat Tours

Enjoy a relaxing boat tour on Lake Sebu. Explore the serene waters and take in the beautiful scenery while learning about the local ecosystem.

Fee: PHP 300-700 for boat rental

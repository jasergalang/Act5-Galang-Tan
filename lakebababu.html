Welcome to Lake Bababu

About Lake Bababu

Lake Bababu, located in the picturesque Dinagat Islands, offers a serene retreat for nature lovers. The clear waters and lush surroundings create an ideal environment for various activities.

Location

Activities

1. Kayaking

Rent a kayak and explore the stunning waters of Lake Bababu. It's a fun way to immerse yourself in nature.

Fee: PHP 300-500 for kayak rental

2. Swimming

Enjoy a refreshing swim in the lake's pristine waters. It's a great way to relax and cool off.

Fee: Free after paying the entrance fee

3. Bird Watching

Observe the diverse bird species around the lake. Consider a guided tour for a more informative experience.

Fee: Generally free; guided tours may cost PHP 200-500

4. Photography

Capture the stunning landscapes and vibrant wildlife of Lake Bababu. Perfect for photography enthusiasts!

Fee: Free
